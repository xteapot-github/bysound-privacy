Bysound data attribution

Thai By Sound Keyboard (Bysound) suggests Thai words using word and word-pair frequencies counted from openly licensed text. Only these counts are bundled in the app. No transcript, web page or sentence text is bundled, and nothing you type is used.

YouTube transcripts (Thai, from app version 0.88)

Part of the Thai counts come from YouTube video transcripts published in two datasets:

  • WangchanLION-Curated by AI Singapore: only rows whose licence is CC BY 4.0 (9,372 videos).
  • YODAS by ESPnet / WAVLab, Thai subset th000, CC BY 3.0 (6,502 videos; every video in our training split, a few of which were then removed by filters).

The full list of channels and videos is here: youtube-thai-v0.88.tsv (tab-separated: source, channel, video URL; YODAS does not record channel names).

Changes: the transcripts were filtered (music, spam and offensive text removed), split into words and counted. Only word and word-pair frequencies are used in the app.
