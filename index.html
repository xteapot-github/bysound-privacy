Bysound Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-25 · Last updated: 2026-09-27 · Applies to app version 0.85.0-alpha

Thai By Sound Keyboard (Bysound) is published by xteapot. xteapot is the developer responsible for the app and for this privacy policy.

Bysound is a private, on-device keyboard. Privacy is the first product priority. The app has no Internet permission and no network path of its own. What you type is processed only on your device to show suggestions. It is never logged as a keystroke history and never uploaded.

What the app processes

  • Characters you type into supported text fields (handled in memory on the device)
  • Suggestion ranking from English and Thai word lists bundled inside the app

Data that stays on your device

  • Settings. Preferences such as language, typing style, auto-correct, haptic feedback, and sound are stored only on your device.
  • Personal dictionary (optional, off by default). If you turn this on in Keyboard settings or Trust Center, committed words and short phrases are saved in app-private files on your device. They are never uploaded. Turning the switch off stops saving and stops using that list. You can clear stored entries in Settings.
  • Suggestion-priority adjustments. An explicit long-press on a suggestion strip word can save a priority adjustment in app-private files on your device. This is separate from the personal dictionary. You can clear it in Settings. These adjustments are never used in password, email, web address (URI), or filter fields.
  • Local diagnostics (on by default). Simple on-device counters and latency samples are shown in Settings or Trust Center. You can turn them off. They stay on your device and are not collected by the developer, xteapot.

What we do not do

On this page, “we” and “us” mean xteapot, the developer of Bysound.

  • No accounts or cloud sync
  • No ads
  • No analytics or crash reporting to us or to third parties
  • No collection of your data off the device, and no sharing of your data with third parties
  • No access to location, contacts, photos, or similar categories
  • Android backups of app data are disabled

Who this app is for

Bysound is intended for users aged 13 and older.
